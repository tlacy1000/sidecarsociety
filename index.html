The Premise
We are not here to be seen.
We are here to connect,
notice,
delight,
and leave the room warmer than we found it.

The Sidecar Standard

Conversation

Ask better questions.
Tell better stories.
Listen like it matters.

Discernment

Curated rooms,
not crowded rooms.
Character over clout.

Warmth

The opposite of transactional.
The antidote to apps,
awkward mixers,
and cold rooms.

Presence

Bring curiosity,
generosity, humor,
nerve, and a willingness
to actually meet people.
