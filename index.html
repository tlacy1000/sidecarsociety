The Premise

Sidecar Society creates intimate social experiences for people who miss meaningful conversation. Through shared dinners, listening rooms, themed gatherings, and beautifully designed spaces, we bring strangers together in ways that feel natural, memorable, and deeply human.

The Sidecar Standard

Conversation

not crowded rooms.

Warmth

The Correspondence Society

Letters Never Sent

An evening built around anonymous letters, quiet reflection, and the words we almost never say aloud.

Thu, Sep 24
7 PM
SoHo
$32
View Details

Guests will be invited to write a letter inspired by one of several prompts:

  • Someone you miss
  • Your younger self
  • A future partner
  • Someone you never thanked
  • Words you never got to say

Letters may be anonymously displayed throughout the evening for others to read, creating a space for unexpected connection and emotional honesty.

Expect:

  • vintage-inspired stationery and writing materials
  • emotionally reflective conversation
